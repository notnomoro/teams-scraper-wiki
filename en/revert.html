Commands / revert
EnglishEspañol

revert

Puts config.json back to the contents of config.prev.json. The aliases are revertir and deshacer.

teams-scraper revert

Each save from init or config leaves the previous version in config.prev.json. revert swaps the two. If there is no previous file, there is nothing to restore.

It does not touch teams.db, the profile, or descargas/.
