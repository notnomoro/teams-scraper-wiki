FAQ

Does it submit the assignment for me?

No. It reads what the account can already see. There is no command to submit, post, or delete.

Does it use my everyday Chrome profile?

No. The profile is perfil inside the data folder, unless a bundled LibreWolf already keeps its own there. You can stay signed out in your normal profile.

Does it send anything to a project server?

No. There is no account, no analytics, and no server. It opens https://teams.cloud.microsoft with Selenium and writes on your disk.

Why is the error still English if I asked for Spanish?

The code is the stable part, so a program can compare it. The sentence is what changes. See Errors.

Can I keep the rows somewhere else?

Yes. Subclass Almacen and pass it to run(store=...). MemoryAlmacen is the same contract without SQLite. The method you cannot skip is save_task.

Does all include chats?

No. all is activity, three assignment lists, and channels. Chats are teams-scraper chats --chat all, and only the ones on the allowed list.

Does a partial read wipe what was stored?

No. --limit and --no-detail do not replace the list JSON. A field that comes back empty, or shorter, leaves the stored value in place.

Where do I see the version?

teams-scraper --version
