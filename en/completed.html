completed

Assignments already turned in.

teams-scraper completed
teams-scraper completed --no-download

What it stores

The tab is Completed. A full read writes completed.json. A card already stored complete is skipped, unless Activity asked for it again.

--no-download stores file names and skips the download on this run. It does not change download_files in the config.
