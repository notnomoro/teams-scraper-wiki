past-due

Assignments past the due date. The alias is pastdue.

teams-scraper past-due
teams-scraper past-due --limit 5

What differs from upcoming

This list does not skip cards already stored. It opens again the ones Teams shows. The stored tab is Past due. A full read writes past_due.json.

The rest of the contract is the same: --limit and --no-detail leave the previous JSON and mark the run partial. An empty field does not wipe a stored value.
